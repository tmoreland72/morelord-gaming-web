# Morelord Game Master

Every DM needs a screen. Morelord Game Master is your in-game Dungeon Master Screen: one window inside Foundry that only the GM sees. Players never open it, but they receive the roll requests you send.

## Setup

Requires Foundry VTT v14 or newer, the D&D 5e system 6.0 or newer, and Morelord Core 0.5.2 or newer. Enable Core and Game Master in your world.

As a GM, click the **Game Master** handle at the bottom of the screen, or press **Alt+G**. You can change the shortcut in Foundry's keybinding settings. The screen opens as a tray above the canvas and below Foundry windows and dialogs, so you can keep working while it is open.

## Player Settings

Choose the characters in scope once. Party requests, selected-token requests, and Bulk Actions use that scope.

## Roll Requests

Build a skill check, ability check, or saving throw with an optional DC, then choose who rolls: the whole party, selected party tokens, or one character. Players receive the request in chat and roll natively with advantage or disadvantage controls. A summary card lists each total and, when a DC is set, who passed.

Turn on **Blind roll** to keep results and summaries visible only to GMs and Assistant GMs. Specialty requests such as Encounter Check, Death Save, and Roll of Fate sit below the builder.

## Bulk Actions

Enter coins once, then split them amongst the party, give the amount to each member, or take coins back. Removal uses personal coins first, then the shared party purse, and reports any shortfall in chat. You can also grant XP and Heroic Inspiration to the party.

## Macros

Drag a macro into the panel to pin it, drag pins to reorder them, and click to run. Pins are saved for the world. Unpinning does not delete the macro.

## Sound

Start a playlist or saved ambience, set every track's volume at once, and stop what is playing from **Now Playing**.

## Triggers

Triggers react to moments in play, such as a Wild Magic surge check after a Sorcerer spell, Sneak Attack damage on a qualifying hit, Lucky Finds after combat, or a World Clock that advances time. Each trigger card shows when it runs and what it does. Turn each trigger on or off with its play and pause control to suit your table. Triggers keep running while the screen is closed.

## Cheat Sheet

Look up D&D 5e conditions, actions, DCs, exhaustion, travel pace, and other quick rules without leaving the game.

## GM Settings

Hide specialty roll requests you do not use and drag them into the order you want. These choices are saved for the world.
